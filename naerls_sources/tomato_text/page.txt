TOMATO STACKING
This is usually required for vegetables with climbing growth habit.
Staking is done to provide support for the tomato plant to climb
and display the leaves for photosynthesis to take place. The
support allows the plant to carry more load without touching the
soil thus enhancing the quality of the fruit. Stake can be made from
bamboo or other available wood.
